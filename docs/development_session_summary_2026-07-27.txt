  Linear fits are applied across the central gauge-block region.

  Peak-to-peak variation: 0.027090 px

  Peak-to-peak variation: 0.000051770 in

Interpretation:
  The subpixel method produced excellent fixed-setup acquisition and
  algorithm repeatability.
