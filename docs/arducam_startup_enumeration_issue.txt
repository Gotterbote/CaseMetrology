Arducam B0499 Startup Enumeration Issue
Date documented: 2026-07-18

Hardware:
- NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano Super Developer Kit
- Arducam B0499 USB3 1.58 MP camera
- USB ID: 04b4:0499
- Serial: Arducam_20231211_0001

Observed behavior:
- Camera does not enumerate automatically following a warm reboot.
- Camera does not enumerate automatically following a full shutdown and cold boot.
- The behavior was reproduced using two physical USB 3 ports.
- No Arducam entry appears in lsusb or the kernel boot log during the failed startup state.
- Physically unplugging and reconnecting the camera restores normal USB 3 and UVC detection.
- Persistent aliases then appear correctly:
  /dev/case-metrology-camera
  /dev/arducam-usb3

Recovery testing:
- uhubctl identified the camera on USB 3 hub 2-1, port 1.
- Software port cycling works when the camera has already enumerated.
- Software port cycling did not reliably recover the camera after it failed to enumerate during startup.
- The experimental systemd recovery service was disabled.

Interim operating procedure:
After Jetson startup, physically reconnect the Arducam and verify that
/dev/case-metrology-camera exists before beginning image acquisition.
