CaseMetrology Gauge-Block Repositioning Repeatability Protocol
Date: 2026-07-24

Purpose:
  Evaluate measurement variation caused by removing and replacing the
  certified gauge block while leaving the camera, lens, lighting, fixture,
  and image-processing settings unchanged.

Reference standard:
  Mitutoyo Grade 0 gauge block
  Certificate number: T26A06624
  Identification number: 253568
  Certified vertical length: 1.0000024 in

Locator fixture:
  A clear Lexan locator plate will fit snugly between the side rails of the
  measurement surface.

  The Lexan plate will contain a notched locating area formed by two
  perpendicular datum faces. A small clearance relief will be provided at
  the inside corner so the gauge-block corner cannot bottom against a cut
  radius. The gauge block must contact both straight datum faces
  simultaneously during seating.

  The gauge block will rest flat on the measurement surface so its
  front-to-back depth plane remains consistent.

  The locator will position the block without clamping, squeezing, lifting,
  or tilting it.

Controlled conditions:
  Camera position remains fixed.
  Lens position and focus remain fixed.
  Camera controls remain locked.
  Backlight position and output remain fixed.
  Lexan locator position remains fixed between the side rails.
  Gauge-block certified dimension remains vertical.
  Gauge-block broad face remains parallel to the camera sensor plane.

Positioning method:
  1. Place the Lexan locator snugly between the measurement-surface rails.
  2. Place the gauge block into the notched locating area.
  3. Gently seat the block against both surfaces of the 90-degree corner.
  4. Confirm that the block rests flat on the measurement surface.
  5. Apply no clamping force that could tilt or distort the block position.

Validation before testing:
  Capture and compare backlight images with and without the Lexan locator.
  Confirm that the Lexan does not create edge glare, reflections, shadows,
  or localized illumination changes near the gauge-block measurement edges.

Repositioning sequence:
  1. Remove the gauge block completely from the locator.
  2. Replace it and gently seat it against both locating surfaces.
  3. Confirm that it rests flat without visible tilt.
  4. Capture one image.
  5. Repeat for 10 independent removal-and-replacement cycles.

Interpretation limits:
  This test evaluates the combined repeatability of the Lexan locator,
  gauge-block seating process, image acquisition, and measurement algorithm.

  It does not by itself establish dimensional accuracy, temperature
  compensation, reproducibility between operators, or total measurement
  uncertainty.
