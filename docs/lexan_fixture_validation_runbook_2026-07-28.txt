CaseMetrology Lexan Locator Validation Runbook
Planned date: 2026-07-28

Purpose:
  Validate that the clear Lexan locator does not adversely alter the
  backlight field, then evaluate gauge-block removal-and-replacement
  repeatability using the validated subpixel measurement method.

=== PHASE 1: PRE-SESSION CHECKS ===
1. Physically reconnect the Arducam if the camera alias is missing.
2. Run:
     ./scripts/run_research_session_preflight.sh
3. Do not proceed unless the final result is PASS.
4. Record ambient temperature near the fixture.
5. Confirm the camera, lens, focus, backlight, and measurement surface
   have not been moved.

=== PHASE 2: NO-LEXAN ILLUMINATION REFERENCE ===
1. Remove the gauge block and Lexan locator from the measurement area.
2. Ensure the backlight field is unobstructed.
3. Capture one controlled image:
     python src/acquisition/capture_research.py \
       --label empty_backlight_without_lexan \
       --count 1
4. Record the image and metadata paths.

=== PHASE 3: LEXAN ILLUMINATION VALIDATION ===
1. Install the unoccupied Lexan locator between the side rails.
2. Do not move the camera, lens, backlight, or measurement surface.
3. Capture one controlled image:
     python src/acquisition/capture_research.py \
       --label empty_backlight_with_lexan \
       --count 1
4. Compare the no-Lexan and with-Lexan images using:
     python src/calibration/compare_illumination.py \
       <without_lexan_image> \
       <with_lexan_image>
5. Inspect both the numerical report and the difference image.
6. Do not proceed if the Lexan creates visible glare, shadows, edge glow,
   or localized intensity changes near the measurement area.

=== PHASE 4: LOCATOR MECHANICAL CHECK ===
1. Confirm the Lexan fits snugly between the side rails without bowing.
2. Confirm the internal-corner relief prevents the gauge-block corner
   from contacting a cut radius.
3. Confirm the gauge block contacts both straight datum faces.
4. Confirm the block rests flat without clamping, lifting, or tilting.
5. Confirm the certified 1.0000024-inch dimension is vertical.

=== PHASE 5: TEN-CYCLE REPOSITIONING TEST ===
1. Run:
     python src/acquisition/capture_repositioning_sequence.py \
       --test-name lexan_gauge_block_repositioning \
       --cycles 10 \
       --mode removal_and_replacement
2. For every prompted cycle:
   a. Remove the gauge block completely.
   b. Replace it in the Lexan locator.
   c. Gently seat it against both datum faces.
   d. Confirm it rests flat.
   e. Press Enter to capture.
3. Do not move the camera, lens, backlight, or Lexan locator during
   the sequence.

=== PHASE 6: SUBPIXEL BATCH ANALYSIS ===
Analyze the 10 captured images using:

  python src/calibration/analyze_subpixel_batch.py \
    <session_image_directory>/*.png \
    --test-name lexan_gauge_block_repositioning \
    --mode repositioning

Review:
  Between-image standard deviation
  Peak-to-peak variation
  Minimum and maximum fitted spans
  Top- and bottom-edge angles
  Edge-angle difference
  Individual-image hashes and deviations

=== INTERPRETATION LIMITS ===
This experiment evaluates the combined repeatability of:
  Lexan locator seating
  Gauge-block removal and replacement
  Operator seating technique
  Image acquisition
  Subpixel edge measurement

It does not by itself establish:
  Absolute dimensional accuracy
  Temperature compensation
  Reproducibility between operators
  Cartridge-case measurement accuracy
  Total measurement uncertainty
