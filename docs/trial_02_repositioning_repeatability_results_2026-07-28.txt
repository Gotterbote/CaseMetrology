The observed between-image standard deviation of 0.000060913 inches and
peak-to-peak variation of 0.000170642 inches are both below the project's

The results indicate that, under the tested conditions, the combined

session.

Acceptance:
Trial 02 is accepted as evidence of single-operator, single-session
fixture repositioning repeatability under the documented configuration.
