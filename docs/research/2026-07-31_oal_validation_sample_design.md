# OAL Validation Sample Design

**Date:** 2026-07-31  
**Status:** Prospective sample-design record

## Available screening population

The study has 235 mixed-length cases. Each case already has a permanent,
laser-etched unique identifier. The existing etched identifier will be used
as the specimen `part_id` throughout screening, reference measurement,
image acquisition, estimator analysis, and evidence reporting.

## Screening design

After the Mitutoyo reference caliper completes calibration testing, all
235 cases will receive one preliminary screening OAL measurement. The
screening measurement will be used only to characterize the available
length distribution and select the independent validation sample.

The initial screening measurement will not serve as the final reference
value for a selected validation case.

## Validation-sample selection

A stratified sample will be selected from the observed distribution rather
than from artificially imposed length groups. Selection will seek coverage
of:

- the shortest available cases;
- cases near the 0.990-inch lower specification limit;
- cases near the 1.000-inch nominal value;
- cases near the 1.010-inch upper specification limit;
- the longest available cases.

The minimum validation sample is 30 cases. A 40-case sample is preferred
when the observed distribution provides adequate coverage.

For each selected validation case, three independent manual-reference
measurements will be taken with complete removal and repositioning between
readings. Their mean will serve as the comparison-reference value, subject
to the reference-instrument calibration record and session verification.

## Research controls

- No case will be renumbered.
- Existing laser-etched identifiers will be preserved exactly.
- Selection will occur after the complete screening distribution is known.
- Cases will not be excluded because their lengths are inconvenient.
- Gaps near a specification limit will be reported as sample limitations.
- The remaining cases will be retained as a reserve population.
- Official computer-vision validation images will be newly collected only
  after sample selection, calibration review, and estimator freeze.

## Evidence status

This document defines the prospective sample-selection method. No official
screening or validation measurements have yet been collected.
