## Status Definitions

- **VERIFIED** — core bibliographic metadata verified from an authoritative or
  publisher-level source.
- **VERIFIED-PARTIAL** — core identity is established, but one or more final
  bibliographic fields still require verification.
- **PRIMARY-STANDARD** — official metrology / standards publication.
- **PRIMARY-MANUFACTURER** — official manufacturer documentation.
- **PENDING-PDF** — final bibliographic detail should be checked against the
  preserved full source before submission.
- **FORMAT-PENDING** — source identity is established, but final citation
  presentation depends on the thesis reference style.

## Citation-Scope Guardrails

- Repository records, calibration certificates, raw data, and Git commits are
  primary CaseMetrology evidence and are not automatically external
  bibliography entries.
- Manufacturer documentation may support hardware specifications but shall not
  be used as independent evidence of CaseMetrology measurement performance.
- NIST SRM 2461 supports forensic optical-measurement context and traceability
  concepts for cartridge-case topography; it does not establish OAL accuracy.
- Historical cartridge-case sources establish prior electro-optical dimensional
  inspection and total-length measurement; they prevent a broad first-ever
  novelty claim.
- JCGM publications support metrology terminology and uncertainty principles;
  they do not supply the project-specific numerical V1 acceptance criteria.
- Bland and Altman support method-agreement methodology; they do not supply the
  CaseMetrology acceptance thresholds.
- Literature describing plausible error mechanisms shall not be used
  retrospectively to assign a causal mechanism to the preserved V1 results.
