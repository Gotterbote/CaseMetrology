# Thesis Citation and Reference Style Decision

**Project:** CaseMetrology  
**Researcher:** Timmy D. Bell  
**Degree:** M.S. Integrated Systems Design & Dynamics, Kansas State University  
**Date:** 2026-09-07  
**Status:** Working thesis-format decision pending any contrary committee instruction

## 1. K-State ETDR Requirement

Kansas State University does not prescribe one universal research citation or
bibliography style for all Electronic Theses, Dissertations, and Reports.

The Graduate School permits citation and bibliographic style appropriate to the
discipline and permits program or committee requirements to control the
specific style used.

K-State ETDR guidance permits author/year citation as well as numbered citation
systems, provided the selected system is used consistently throughout the
manuscript.

## 2. CaseMetrology Citation Decision

Unless the major professor, committee, or Integrated Systems Design & Dynamics
program provides a contrary requirement, the CaseMetrology thesis will use a
consistent author-year citation system with APA-like reference formatting.

The final thesis-facing citation form will therefore use conventions such as:

- `(Wang et al., 2024)`
- `(Bland & Altman, 1986)`
- `(Hemming, 2007; Lazzari & Iuculano, 2004)`
- `(JCGM, 2008)` or another consistent corporate-author abbreviation after
  final JCGM formatting is established.

The current uppercase bracketed citation tokens are temporary research and
audit identifiers only.

Examples:

- `[WANG ET AL. 2024]`
- `[BLAND AND ALTMAN 1986]`
- `[JCGM 100:2008]`

These tokens shall be retained in preserved working artifacts but replaced in
the thesis-facing chapter versions.

## 3. References Section

The final References section will:

- contain every external source cited in the thesis;
- contain no uncited external bibliography entries;
- be alphabetized by author or corporate author;
- use one consistent author-year bibliographic format;
- use italics, capitalization, volume/issue information, page ranges, DOI,
  URL, and access-date treatment consistently according to the selected style;
- use hanging indents in the final Word document;
- permit single spacing within each bibliographic entry, with separation
  between entries consistent with K-State ETDR requirements.

## 4. Corporate and Standards Authors

Corporate and standards publications will be cited consistently by the
responsible organization.

Examples include:

- Joint Committee for Guides in Metrology;
- National Institute of Standards and Technology;
- NVIDIA Corporation; and
- Arducam.

Abbreviations such as JCGM and NIST shall not be introduced inconsistently
between in-text citations and the References section.

## 5. Historical Publication-Year Guardrail

The Coleman and Swinth cartridge-case inspection source retains its current
working 1977 citation token until the distinction between the 1977
conference/article date and the 1978 SPIE volume-publication date is resolved.

No automated citation conversion shall silently change that year.

## 6. Source-Identity Guardrail

Citation-style conversion is formatting work.

It shall not:

- change which source supports a claim;
- merge distinct sources;
- introduce uncited sources;
- remove sources because they are cited only once;
- change source years merely to satisfy formatting software; or
- alter the evidence-status limitations preserved in the bibliography matrix.

## 7. Committee Override

If the major professor, committee, or program specifies another citation style
before submission, that requirement supersedes this working decision.

Any resulting conversion shall be performed systematically across both in-text
citations and the References section and followed by a bidirectional citation
coverage audit.
