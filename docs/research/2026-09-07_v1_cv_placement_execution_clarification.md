# V1 Formal CV Placement Execution Clarification

Date documented: 2026-09-07
Project: CaseMetrology
Scope: Formal OAL Validation V1
Operator: Timmy D. Bell

## Purpose

This record documents a factual clarification of the physical specimen
placement conditions used during the already-completed V1 formal CV
validation acquisition.

This record does not modify the frozen V1 protocol, measurement data,
analysis rules, acceptance criteria, or formal results.

## V1 Measurement-Surface Placement

During formal V1 CV acquisition:

- specimens were placed freely on the backlit measurement surface within
  the camera viewing window;
- no specimen-positioning fixture was used;
- no rails, stops, pockets, locating pins, guides, or other mechanical
  locating features constrained specimen position;
- no explicit placement boundary within the usable camera field was imposed;
- specimens were generally placed with the cartridge-case long axis
  approximately vertical in the image;
- base and mouth orientation were kept consistent across placements;
- specimens were not intentionally reversed 180 degrees between
  measurements;
- exact image-plane position and small angular placement differences were
  otherwise unconstrained.

## Between-Round Handling

After completion of a CV round:

- each specimen was fully removed from the measurement surface;
- specimens were returned to the gridded plastic staging/storage container;
- before the next round, specimens were removed and reordered according to
  the next frozen randomized CV run order;
- every formal CV observation therefore involved a new independent physical
  placement.

The gridded plastic container served only as specimen storage and staging.
It was not part of the measurement fixture or measurement geometry.

## Camera and Illumination

The camera and backlight configuration were not intentionally changed
between the three formal CV rounds.

## Interpretation

Accordingly, the formal V1 repositioning analysis characterizes
placement-dependent repeatability under independent, largely unconstrained
specimen placement with consistent gross base-to-mouth orientation.

It shall not be described as repeatability within a dedicated mechanical
specimen-positioning fixture.

Any later introduction of mechanical locating features or constrained
placement shall be treated as a post-validation engineering modification
and not as part of the V1 formal system.
