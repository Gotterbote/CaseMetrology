CaseMetrology Repositioning Session Integrity Verification Protocol

Protocol date:
2026-07-28

Purpose:
Define a reproducible, version-controlled procedure for validating a
gauge-block repositioning acquisition session before dimensional or
repeatability analysis is performed.

Principle:
Verification establishes that the recorded acquisition evidence is
complete, internally consistent, readable, and unchanged. Verification
is not dimensional analysis and does not establish measurement accuracy.

Scope:
This protocol applies to sessions generated by:

  src/acquisition/capture_repositioning_sequence.py

The verification software shall use the session metadata JSON as the
authoritative index of acquired images and expected acquisition state.

AUTOMATED VERIFICATION REQUIREMENTS

1. Metadata structure
The verifier shall confirm that required session, camera, control, cycle,
and capture fields are present and usable.

2. Acquisition completeness
The verifier shall confirm:
- cycles_requested equals cycles_completed;
- the number of capture records equals cycles_completed;
- cycle numbers are sequential and unique;
- image paths and filenames agree with the metadata.

3. Source-code traceability
The verifier shall report:
- the Git commit recorded at acquisition;
- whether the working tree was clean at acquisition;
- the Git commit containing the verification method.

The acquisition session fails verification if the metadata records a
dirty working tree.

4. Camera configuration
For the current CaseMetrology rig, the verifier shall require:
- device: /dev/case-metrology-camera;
- width: 1456 pixels;
- height: 1088 pixels;
- frame rate: 44 frames per second;
- pixel format: YUYV;
- warmup frames: 20.

Any intentional future configuration change requires a documented
protocol and software revision before additional official acquisition.

5. Camera-control consistency
The verifier shall compare:
- expected camera controls;
- controls recorded before acquisition;
- controls recorded after acquisition;
- controls recorded for every individual capture.

All required control dictionaries must match exactly.

6. File existence and readability
Each image listed in the metadata shall:
- exist at the recorded path;
- be readable as an image;
- have the expected width and height.

7. File-integrity checks
For every image, the verifier shall confirm:
- actual byte size equals the recorded byte size;
- recalculated SHA-256 digest equals the digest recorded at acquisition.

8. Record uniqueness
The verifier shall identify duplicate cycle numbers, duplicate image
paths, or other contradictory capture records.

9. Standardized outputs
The verifier shall generate:
- a human-readable verification report;
- a SHA-256 evidence manifest;
- a process exit status of zero for PASS and nonzero for FAIL.

The evidence manifest shall include the original metadata, all acquired
images, and the generated verification report. It shall not include
itself.

MANUAL PREREQUISITES AND REVIEWS

The following items are scientifically important but are not established
by file-integrity verification alone:

1. Gauge-block orientation
The certified 1.0000024-inch dimension must be documented as vertical,
with the approximately 1.375-inch dimension horizontal.

2. Physical seating
The operator must confirm that the block was removed completely,
replaced, seated against both datum faces, and resting flat during every
cycle.

3. Optical setup
The camera, lens, focus, backlight, and Lexan locator must remain
unchanged during the sequence.

4. Environmental observation
Ambient or fixture temperature shall be recorded with the instrument
type and measurement classification. Approximate readings must not be
represented as traceable temperature-metrology evidence.

5. Diagnostic image review
After integrity verification and subpixel analysis, diagnostic overlays
must be inspected to confirm that the fitted measurement edges follow
the gauge block rather than the Lexan locator, shadows, scratches, or
other image features.

PASS AND FAIL LOGIC

Automated integrity verification passes only when every required
automated check passes.

Overall experimental acceptance additionally requires that the manual
prerequisites and diagnostic reviews be documented and accepted.

A failed automated check shall stop dimensional analysis until the cause
is investigated and documented. Original evidence shall not be edited
to force a passing result.

EVIDENCE-PRESERVATION REQUIREMENTS

- Original images and acquisition metadata shall not be modified.
- Corrections or interpretations shall be added as separate records.
- Exploratory, invalidated, or orientation-error trials shall remain
  preserved with an explicit disposition.
- Verification reports and manifests shall identify the specific
  acquisition session.
- Official results shall be associated with specific Git commits.

INTERPRETATION LIMITS

A passing integrity verification does not independently establish:
- absolute dimensional accuracy;
- dimensional repeatability;
- correct gauge-block orientation;
- correct edge detection;
- temperature compensation;
- measurement uncertainty;
- reproducibility between operators;
- cartridge-case measurement performance.

THESIS REPORTING GUIDANCE

A concise methods statement may report:

"Each repositioning dataset underwent automated pre-analysis integrity
verification. The procedure checked acquisition completeness, camera
configuration and control consistency, image readability and dimensions,
recorded file size, and SHA-256 file integrity. Gauge-block orientation,
physical seating, environmental observations, and diagnostic edge-fit
overlays were reviewed separately because these conditions cannot be
established through file-integrity checks alone."

Full reports and manifests may be retained in the project repository or
included as supplemental material rather than reproduced in full in the
main thesis text.
