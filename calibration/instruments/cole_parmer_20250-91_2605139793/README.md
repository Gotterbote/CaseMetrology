## Intake observations

- The report, rear labels, front marking, and calibration sticker consistently
  identify the same meter.
- The report documents calibration by simulated thermocouple input. It verifies
  the meter electronics and cold-junction behavior, but not an attached probe.
- The separately supplied Cole-Parmer 08519-54 Type-T self-adhesive probes are
  not individually calibrated by report 1817067.
- The temperature system is intended for environmental and stability
  monitoring; these records do not support claiming that the probes themselves
  are NIST calibrated.
