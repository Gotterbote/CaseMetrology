Lexan Optical Comparison — Preliminary Research Interpretation

Test date: 2026-07-28

Purpose:
Evaluate whether installing the Lexan fixture surface materially changes
illumination within the intended unobstructed measurement region.

Controlled acquisition conditions:
- Camera: Arducam B0499 USB3 global-shutter camera
- Resolution: 1456 x 1088
- Pixel format: YUYV
- Frame rate: 44 fps
- Exposure mode: Manual
- Exposure value: 5
- Gain: 100
- Automatic white balance: Disabled
- Camera settings verified unchanged before and after capture

Fixed ROI:
- x = 500:1300
- y = 350:900
- ROI size = 800 x 550 pixels

Results:
- Mean grayscale without Lexan: 225.1456
- Mean grayscale with Lexan: 225.0625
- Mean grayscale change: -0.0831
- Mean percent change: -0.0369%
- Mean absolute pixel difference: 0.8864
- RMSE: 1.2830
- 95th-percentile absolute difference: 3.0000

Preliminary interpretation:
The Lexan did not produce a practically meaningful change in mean
illumination within the selected unobstructed ROI. The central measurement
region remained optically stable under the tested conditions.

Limitations:
This result is based on one paired capture and does not independently
establish repeatability. The visible Lexan or fixture boundary must remain
outside the dimensional measurement ROI because it may create false edges.

Conclusion:
The Lexan is provisionally suitable for continued fixture testing within
the defined central ROI. Removal-and-replacement repeatability testing is
still required.
