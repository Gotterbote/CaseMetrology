Disposition reason:
The gauge block was installed approximately 90 degrees from the orientation
specified in the approved repositioning protocol.

The protocol requires the certified 1.0000024-inch dimension to be vertical.
During this trial, the longer approximately 1.375-inch dimension was oriented
vertically and the certified dimension was oriented horizontally.

Permitted use:
This dataset may be retained as exploratory evidence concerning:
- Gauge-block removal and replacement
- Mechanical locator seating behavior
- Operator seating consistency
- Image-acquisition stability
- Pixel-level positional and edge repeatability

Prohibited use:
This dataset must not be used as:
- The official certified gauge-block repositioning trial
- A certified 1.0000024-inch dimensional calibration
- Evidence of absolute dimensional accuracy
- Final acceptance evidence for the Lexan locator
- A source of inch-based results using the one-inch vertical calibration

Required corrective action:
Repeat the full 10-cycle experiment with:
- The certified 1.0000024-inch dimension vertical
- The approximately 1.375-inch dimension horizontal
- The gauge block appearing wider than it is tall
- Camera, lens, backlight, and Lexan locator unchanged

Evidence-preservation instruction:
Do not alter, rename, overwrite, or delete the original images or session
metadata. This disposition record supplements the original acquisition
record and does not replace it.
