## Interpretation

Controlled mechanical seating using the existing Lexan locator substantially
reduced placement-associated variability relative to the free-placement V1
condition.

However, controlled positioning did not reduce variability to the previously
observed fixed-specimen noise floor.

The controlled placements continued to exhibit measurable variation in
centroid position and image-plane angle, indicating that the current locator
and seating rule do not establish a single unique physical pose.

Several valid placements also contained unusually large within-placement
frame-to-frame changes, particularly P04-R1, P09-R1, and P10. These observations
were retained because no operator-defined invalidating event was identified
before measurement analysis.

The anomalous frames should be examined diagnostically for segmentation,
component, or endpoint behavior before deciding whether to proceed to the
60-specimen V2-CP1 study.

No estimator, segmentation threshold, calibration, software, camera, lens,
or measurement-system parameter was modified based on these results.
