## Interpretation

The fixed-specimen measurement variation was much smaller than the
formal V1 independent-placement pairwise P95 of 0.004831 in.

Under the blocked/off baseline, fixed-specimen P95 pairwise variation
was approximately 45 times smaller than the V1 independent-placement
result.

This provides evidence that ordinary frame-to-frame
camera/illumination/segmentation variation is not sufficient to explain
the magnitude of the V1 independent-placement variability.

The present diagnostic does not establish that placement caused the V1
errors. Controlled repositioning is required to test that hypothesis.

No estimator, calibration, threshold, software, camera, lens, or
measurement-system parameter was changed based on these results.
